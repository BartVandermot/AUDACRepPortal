Import

Your AUDAC projects

Upload the AUDAC projects from your own CRM (QuickRep, Salesforce, HubSpot, Excel…). They show up on My territory, next to the projects already registered with AVL, so AUDAC can back you up and plan stock. Upload again whenever you like: projects are updated by their CRM id, never duplicated. Open projects are also added to AUDAC's CRM, so nobody types them twice. Uploading does not register a project: registration (and the dealer discount) only goes through AVL's website.

1. Upload your export

An Excel (.xlsx) or CSV file with one project per row and column names in the first row. Every row needs a project name and the project's id in your CRM.

2. Match your columns

Tell us which column holds each piece of information. We pre-filled what we recognised; this is saved for your firm, so next time it's already done.

3. Check & import

Rows without a project name or CRM id are skipped. When your file has a brand or manufacturer column, only AUDAC rows are imported.
