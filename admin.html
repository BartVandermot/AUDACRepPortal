Pipeline

Pulls Opportunities (US/Canada only, per the Opportunity's own Country Lookup field) live from Dynamics 365 — Open, Won, and Lost alike, so a deal's outcome stays visible once it closes instead of disappearing from the sync. Since almost every opportunity is filed under Intellimix as the customer, rep firm is resolved via, in order: the Involved Installer account, the CRM contact's own resolved-or-overridden firm, then the free-typed Location field. Open deals are tagged with the month they were created in CRM (a stable, one-row-ever assignment, not a fresh snapshot every sync); Won/Lost deals are tagged with the month they actually closed, so a win/loss lands on the scorecard for when it really happened. The file upload below stays available as a fallback for a specific past month.
A contact a sync couldn't resolve to a rep firm is flagged "Blocking pipeline sync" on the Accounts & Contacts page — fix it there by assigning that contact a rep firm.
