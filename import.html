Rep firm

Upload the activities your firm logged in its own CRM (Salesforce, HubSpot, Dynamics, Excel…) instead of typing them in one by one. Each activity is filed under the reporting month of its own date.

1. Upload your export

An Excel (.xlsx) or CSV file with one activity per row and column names in the first row. Max 2,000 activities per file.

2. Match your columns

Tell us which column in your file holds each piece of information. We pre-filled what we could recognise; this is saved for your firm, so next time it's already done.

3. Match your values

Your CRM uses its own words for activity and contact types. Choose the AUDAC equivalent for each one.

4. Check & import

We check every row before anything is saved: missing or unreadable fields, months that have already been finalized, activities that are already in the portal, and which accounts we recognise from the AUDAC CRM.
